About World of Kids

Play that is fun now and useful later

World of Kids is a children's toy brand built on one idea: playtime should bring joy and help children grow.

We bring together educational toys, creative and building sets, puzzles, dolls, action figures, soft toys, remote-control toys and interactive learning games. They are made for children aged 1 to 12, so there is something for every stage.

Our goal is to make it easy for parents and families in Bangladesh to find toys that are fun, age-appropriate and fairly priced.
